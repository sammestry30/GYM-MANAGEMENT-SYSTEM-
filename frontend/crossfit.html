High-Intensity Crossfit Training

CrossFit is a lifestyle characterized by safe, effective exercise and sound nutrition. It involves constantly varied functional movements performed at high intensity.

WOD Example: "Helen"

  • 3 Rounds For Time:
  • 400 meter Run
  • 21 Kettlebell Swings (24/16 kg)
  • 12 Pull-ups

WOD Example: "Fran"

  • 21-15-9 Reps For Time:
  • Thrusters (95/65 lb)
  • Pull-ups

WOD Example: "Murph"

  • For Time (with a 20/14 lb weight vest):
  • 1 mile Run, 100 Pull-ups, 200 Push-ups, 300 Squats, 1 mile Run
