Strength Training Plan

This program is built around core compound lifts to maximize strength gains. Focus on heavy weights and lower repetitions.

Day 1: Upper Body Strength

  • Barbell Bench Press: 5 sets of 5 reps
  • Bent-Over Barbell Rows: 5 sets of 5 reps
  • Overhead Press: 3 sets of 5-8 reps
  • Pull-ups: 3 sets to failure

Day 2: Lower Body Strength

  • Barbell Squats: 5 sets of 5 reps
  • Deadlifts: 1 set of 5 reps (warm-up sets first)
  • Leg Press: 3 sets of 8-10 reps

Day 3: Full Body Power

  • Power Cleans: 5 sets of 3 reps
  • Incline Bench Press: 3 sets of 5-8 reps
  • Front Squats: 3 sets of 5-8 reps
