Olympic Weightlifting Plan

A beginner-focused plan for learning the Snatch and Clean & Jerk. Focus on perfect form over heavy weight.

Day 1: Snatch & Squats

  • Snatch: 6 sets of 3 reps (technique focus)
  • Overhead Squats: 3 sets of 5 reps
  • Back Squats: 5 sets of 5 reps

Day 2: Clean & Jerk & Press

  • Clean & Jerk: 6 sets of 1+1 reps (1 clean, 1 jerk)
  • Front Squats: 5 sets of 3 reps
  • Push Press: 5 sets of 5 reps

Day 3: Volume & Strength

  • Power Snatch: 5 sets of 3 reps
  • Power Clean: 5 sets of 3 reps
  • Bent-Over Rows: 3 sets of 8-10 reps
