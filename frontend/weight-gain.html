Weight Gain (Bulking) Plan

This 4-day split focuses on hypertrophy (muscle growth) by targeting specific muscle groups each day. Ensure you are in a caloric surplus.

Day 1: Chest & Triceps

  • Barbell Bench Press: 4 sets of 6-10 reps
  • Incline Dumbbell Press: 3 sets of 8-12 reps
  • Tricep Pushdowns: 3 sets of 10-15 reps

Day 2: Back & Biceps

  • Pull-ups: 3 sets to failure
  • Bent-Over Barbell Rows: 4 sets of 6-10 reps
  • Barbell Curls: 3 sets of 8-12 reps

Day 3: Legs

  • Squats: 4 sets of 6-10 reps
  • Leg Press: 3 sets of 8-12 reps
  • Romanian Deadlifts: 3 sets of 8-12 reps

Day 4: Shoulders & Abs

  • Overhead Barbell Press: 4 sets of 6-10 reps
  • Dumbbell Lateral Raises: 3 sets of 12-15 reps
  • Hanging Leg Raises: 3 sets to failure
