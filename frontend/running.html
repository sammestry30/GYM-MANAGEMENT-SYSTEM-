Beginner Running Plan

This 3-day per week plan is designed to get you from walking to jogging consistently. Focus on completing the time, not the speed.

Week 1-2: Walk/Jog Intervals

  • Day 1: 5 min walk, then 6 reps of (90s jog, 2 min walk)
  • Day 2: Rest or cross-train
  • Day 3: 5 min walk, then 8 reps of (60s jog, 90s walk)

Week 3-4: Increase Jog Time

  • Day 1: 5 min walk, then 2 reps of (5 min jog, 3 min walk)
  • Day 2: Rest or cross-train
  • Day 3: 5 min walk, then jog for 15 minutes straight
