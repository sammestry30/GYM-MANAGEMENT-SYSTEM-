Fat Loss Plan

This plan combines full-body resistance training with HIIT to maximize calorie burn and preserve muscle mass. A proper diet is crucial for success.

Day 1: Full Body Strength & HIIT

  • Goblet Squats: 3 sets of 10-15 reps
  • Dumbbell Bench Press: 3 sets of 10-15 reps
  • Dumbbell Rows: 3 sets of 10-15 reps
  • HIIT on Treadmill: 10 rounds of 30s sprint, 60s walk

Day 2: Full Body Strength & HIIT

  • Dumbbell Lunges: 3 sets of 10-12 reps per leg
  • Push-ups: 3 sets to failure
  • Lat Pulldowns: 3 sets of 10-15 reps
  • HIIT on Bike: 15 rounds of 20s sprint, 40s easy pace

Day 3: Full Body Strength & LISS

  • Leg Press: 3 sets of 15-20 reps
  • Seated Cable Row: 3 sets of 10-15 reps
  • Plank: 3 sets, hold for max time
  • LISS Cardio: 30-45 minutes of incline walking
